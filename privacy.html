Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

ForceForge is a certification revision application. This policy explains the information processed when you use the ForceForge website or mobile application.

ForceForge is operated by Kieran Daly, who is the data controller. Privacy enquiries can be sent to shunraw@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Your email address and authentication information.
  • The date and time you confirmed that you meet our minimum age requirement. We do not store your date of birth.
  • Quiz answers, results, progress and selected certifications.
  • Information you provide when contacting support.

How information is used

We use this information to create and secure accounts, synchronise learning progress, deliver support, prevent abuse and improve reliability.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database and backend services. Cloudflare Turnstile may be used to prevent automated abuse. These providers process information under their own privacy terms. ForceForge does not use an advertising SDK or process information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Account and revision information is retained while your account is active, except where limited retention is necessary for security, fraud prevention, disputes or legal compliance. You can permanently delete your account and associated revision data inside the app. You can also use our web deletion request.

Age requirement

ForceForge is available only to people aged 16 or over. During registration, your date of birth is checked on your device to determine eligibility and is not stored. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we will take steps to close it and delete associated personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.

Your choices and contact

You may ask for access, correction or deletion of your information by emailing shunraw@gmail.com. Rights and available remedies vary by location.
